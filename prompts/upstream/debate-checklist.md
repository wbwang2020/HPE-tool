Use these four upstream critique categories explicitly. For each category, write at least one concrete question or finding with page/quote evidence, or state "not applicable" with a reason.

1. Algorithm/model design soundness
   - Is the algorithm logically consistent with the problem?
   - Is the model architecture justified?
   - Are there hidden design flaws or unstated assumptions?
   - Is the complexity cost acceptable and correctly analyzed?

2. Innovation rationale
   - Is the innovation clearly stated?
   - Is it supported by a concrete difference from the closest work?
   - Is the difference scientifically or practically valuable?
   - Is the claimed "first"/"new paradigm" positioned against prior reviews/works?

3. Content reliability
   - Is the experimental/characterization design adequate?
   - Are data sources and processing traceable?
   - Are results statistically/experimentally significant?
   - Do conclusions follow from the evidence?

4. Other
   - Is the theoretical foundation sound?
   - Is the method description reproducible?
   - Are failure cases, limitations and negative results discussed?
   - Are ethics/safety/dual-use issues handled concretely?
