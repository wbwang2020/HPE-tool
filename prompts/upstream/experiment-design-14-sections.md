1. Basic information: project name, purpose, domain, date.
2. Experimental objectives and hypotheses: what is tested; falsifiable predictions.
3. Experimental design and variables: independent/dependent/control variables; inclusion/exclusion; levels and repeats.
4. Sample size and power: calculation basis, effect size, alpha/power, sampling method.
5. Control/comparison groups: choice, fairness, blinding/randomization where relevant.
6. Materials/instruments/protocol: standards, calibration, environment, operator training.
7. Data collection: instruments, sampling rate, raw data format, metadata, missing data.
8. Data processing: cleaning, normalization, outlier policy, versioning.
9. Statistical analysis: test choice, assumptions, multiple comparisons, effect size, confidence intervals.
10. Validity and reliability: internal/external validity, repeatability, reproducibility.
11. Risks and mitigation: technical, safety, ethical, schedule; stop rules.
12. Resource and timeline: equipment, cost, personnel, milestones.
13. Expected results and interpretation: what result would support/refute each hypothesis.
14. Limitations and reporting: what cannot be concluded, preregistration/reporting plan.
